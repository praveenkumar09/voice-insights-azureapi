# How to use your hands: the 4-minute EXCO speech

Companion to `Presenting-EXCO-4min-hands-guide.mp4`. The video is an **animated guide**: the presenter is an illustration, not a real person. Watch it, then practise in a mirror or record yourself on your phone.

## The six rules

| # | Rule | How |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Home position** | Hands loosely together at belly-button height. Return here between gestures. |
| 2 | **Gesture inside the box** | Keep hands between your shoulders and your belt. Never above the eyes, never behind you. |
| 3 | **One gesture, one idea** | One gesture per sentence. Do not wave your hands all the time. |
| 4 | **Count on your fingers** | For lists of three, four or five, show them, one finger each. |
| 5 | **Stillness is power** | On each WOW line: hands down, feet planted, three seconds of silence. |
| 6 | **Look, then gesture** | Look at one person, finish the sentence, then move. Never gesture while reading the screen. |

## The gestures used in the video

| Gesture | What to do | Use it for |
|---|---|---|
| Home position | Hands together at the belly | Start, change of topic |
| Open palms | Palms up, in front of you, relaxed | Explaining, inviting, "the answer" |
| Wide welcome | Arms widen gently to include the room | "Every campaign", "the way Singapore talks" |
| Palms down | Flat hands, slowly lowering | Calm, "they wait", "stop", "same shape every time" |
| Count on fingers | One finger per item, slowly | Three things, four jobs, five questions |
| One finger | Index finger up, slightly tilted | "Spark doesn't wait", "one click" |
| Hand to chest | Flat hand on the chest | "Why we exist", "only a person can approve" |
| Offer with both hands | Both hands forward, as if handing over a folder | Proposal, advice pack, "the recommendation" |
| Point to the screen | Arm out toward the screen, then back | "Start the video" |
| Hands together at chest | Fingertips together | The close: "Spark suggests. The advisor decides." |
| **Stand still** | Hands at your sides, feet planted, no movement | All five WOW lines and the last two lines |

## The five WOW lines (stand still for three seconds)

1. "The conversation is the product. And we have been throwing it away."
2. "Ninety seconds. Nobody typed a word."
3. "It doesn't just know our products. It knows what we are running today."
4. "One conversation. Thirty seconds. And the evening is theirs again."
5. "It is a flight simulator for a conversation. And the passenger is real."

## Timing: read this

The speech as written is **746 words**, not the 560 the script header says. In the video it runs about **5 minutes 10 seconds** at a clear, steady pace with short pauses. To reach 4:00 either:
- cut the items on the script's "If you run over" list, and shorten the longer sentences in parts 3 and 4, or
- tell the organiser you need five minutes.

## Practice plan (20 minutes)

1. Watch the video once with sound, once with the sound off (just the hands).
2. Stand in front of a mirror and do the six gestures slowly, without speaking.
3. Record yourself on your phone doing part 1 (0:00 to 0:50). Compare it with the video.
4. Practise the five stillness moments on their own: say the line, hands down, count to three silently.
5. Run the whole speech once, with a timer.

## What the video does not show

- Walking. Walk to the centre before you start, then stay planted. Move only at a change of topic.
- Eye contact. Look at different people across the room, one sentence each.
- Your own style. Make each gesture smaller or bigger to suit you; the pattern matters more than the exact shape.
