## Before you start (not on the clock)

- **Room:** quiet, TV off. Headphones or a headset help, because Juno's own voice can leak into the microphone and confuse it.
- **Sign in first**, and open the Mr Tan session that already has a finished analysis, on the **Live vs final** page. Keep a second tab on the **Advice pack**.
- **Test the microphone and sound once** in the same room, with voice on, ten minutes before.
- **Pace:** say one sentence, then wait. Juno starts answering about 4–8 seconds after you stop, so fill that gap with narration (the narration lines are written in below).
- **Do a full dry run** the day before, by voice, including one what-if. The latest speech changes (pause handling) are deployed but not yet fully re-verified.
- **Do not click "Download PDF" live.** It opens the print dialog and can freeze the browser. Show the on-screen tabs instead.

## 0:00–1:00 · The problem and the promise

**Say**

> "Our advisors spend the meeting listening and the evening on paperwork: the fact-find, the record of advice, the follow-up, the CRM notes. What gets written down is often only what they remember.
>
> Voice Insights turns the conversation itself into the work. It listens, it builds the customer picture, it runs a team of AI agents to suggest products, and it produces the paperwork. The advisor stays in charge at every step.
>
> In ten minutes I'll take you from one customer conversation to a finished advice pack — and then I'll let you question the AI."

**Demo:** home screen, showing the three capture modes.

## 1:00–2:30 · Capture: three ways in

**Say**

> "An advisor can use it in three ways. **With the customer**, it listens live and builds the customer's family, hopes and worries as they speak. **After the meeting**, they dictate a summary. Or they can **debrief with Juno**, who plays back what she understood and asks about anything that is missing.
>
> It copes with how people really talk: English mixed with Mandarin, hesitations, pauses. ID and card numbers are masked before anything is stored."

**Demo:** open **After the meeting**, click the microphone and say:

> *"I met Mr Tan today. He is thirty, a software engineer, married with two children. He's worried about his home loan and his children's education."*

**Say while the transcript and life map fill in**

> "Look at the life map on the right. People, hopes, worries and protection ideas appear as I speak, and each one shows the words it came from."

Then stop the recording.

## 2:30–4:00 · Analysis: a team of agents, not one chatbot

**Say**

> "When the advisor finishes, eleven specialist steps work on the customer: needs, risk, affordability, customer type, product scoring, checking against our own product documents, and a compliance check. It's a pipeline, not a single answer, and every step's input and output is stored, so it can be audited afterwards."

**Demo:** open the finished analysis, **Live vs final**.

**Say**

> "On the left is what the live copilot guessed during the conversation. On the right is the full analysis. The live view is fast; the full analysis is the one of record.
>
> Notice the wording. It says **suggestion**, never recommendation, and it describes relevance in plain words — 'highly relevant' — never a percentage. That is deliberate and follows the MAS guidance: these are suggestions for the advisor to review, not advice."

---

## 4:00–6:30 · The wow moment: talk it through with Juno

**Say**

> "Advisors shouldn't have to trust a black box. So they can question it, out loud, like a colleague who was in the room."

**Demo:** scroll to the Juno panel, make sure **Voice on**, tap to start. Ask, one at a time, waiting for each answer to finish:

1. *"Why did you put Secure Guard Term first?"*
2. *"Isn't that a bit generic?"* (a challenge)
3. *"Can you drop it and lead with the investment plan?"*

**Say while she is thinking (the 4–8 second gap)**

> "She's reading this customer's file and the product documents. Watch the small chips under each sentence: tap one and you see exactly where that statement came from — what the customer said, the agents' analysis, a compliance finding, or a product document. If a sentence has no source behind it, it is visibly marked."

**Point out after question 3**

> "She won't change the list because I asked. She says so plainly and explains what that would leave uncovered. The analysis is the record, and a chat can't quietly rewrite it. That's what protects us in an audit."

## 6:30–8:00 · "What if…?"

**Say**

> "Customers' situations change, so the advisor can ask what would be different."

**Demo, by voice:** *"What if Mr Tan had one more child?"*

Juno reads back the changed fact ("dependants go from 2 to 3 … shall I run that through the analysis?"). Say **"Yes, go ahead."** The progress bar runs for about 20–40 seconds.

**Say while it runs**

> "She isn't guessing. She is re-running the real analysis on a scratch copy of the profile. The customer's actual profile and the original suggestions are untouched, and this scenario is labelled as explored, not real."

**When the comparison appears**

> "Original beside the new result, product by product, with the reason it moved or didn't. Here the order holds, and she says so honestly. With a fuller product catalogue you would see products move."

*(If you want to show movement, tell them upfront that the demo catalogue is small.)*

## Likely questions, with honest answers

**Is this "advice" under MAS rules?**
No. It produces suggestions for the advisor to review; the advisor decides. The wording, the sources and the audit trail were designed around that, but Compliance should review it formally before any customer-facing use.

**Can it make things up?**
It is restricted to the customer's file and our product documents. Statements without a source are marked, and it says "that isn't in my notes" rather than guess. It's still a pilot-grade control, not a finished one.

**Where does the data go, and who sees it?**
ID and card numbers are masked before storage. The services run on our Azure OpenAI resource. Hosting, retention and approval of the services are decisions to take with IT security before a pilot.

**How fast is it?**
Juno starts answering about 4–8 seconds after a question, and a what-if takes about 20–40 seconds. What I'm showing is the real speed, not a recording. A plain one-breath question also waits about a second of silence before she starts, so she doesn't cut an advisor off.

**Why not just use a general chatbot?**
It isn't tied to our customer file or our product documents, it shows no sources, and it doesn't produce our paperwork.

**What is the business value?**
I haven't measured it yet. The pilot is how we measure time saved per case and follow-up quality. I would not quote a number today.

**Does it work in a noisy room or in other languages?**
It handles English, Mandarin, Malay and Tamil for the reports; spoken Q&A has been tested in English and Mandarin. A noisy room or a TV makes it less reliable, so I'd use a quiet room and headphones.

## If something goes wrong during the demo

| Problem | What to do |
|---|---|
| Juno mishears a question | Type it in the box under the conversation. The answer path is the same. |
| The what-if is slow | Say "that's the real analysis running" and keep talking about the comparison. |
| The page signs you out | Sign back in. The analysis and discussion are saved. |
| Juno cuts in or repeats | Stop, say "let me ask that again," and use the typed box. |
| No sound | Check Voice is on; the answers also appear as text with sources. |
| Silence for more than 10 seconds | Say "she's checking the documents" and wait; if still nothing, type the question. |

---

## Things to be upfront about

- The demo product catalogue is small (two main products), so what-if results often keep the same order.
- This is a working prototype for a pilot, not a finished product. The recent voice improvements are on the `phase-6` branch and are not committed yet.
- The animated Juno avatar idea was dropped; don't mention it as coming.
- "Make this the new suggestion" from a what-if is not built. A what-if never changes the record.
