Azure OpenAI in Azure AI Foundry  four model deployments, in our subscription
gpt-4.1
Chat · vision

Reasoning model behind Spark and the analysis agents. Extracts the fact-find, writes suggestions and proposals, and reads campaign posters.

gpt-4o-transcribe
Speech to text

Turns the advisor's live speech into text, across English, Mandarin, Malay, Tamil, Thai, Filipino and Vietnamese, including mixed languages.

gpt-4o-mini-tts
Text to speech

Gives Spark its natural voice (Coral and Ash) in every supported language, with a tone that fits each line.

text-embedding-3-large
Embeddings

Turns text into 3072-dimension vectors to search product documents and to match a customer's words to campaigns.
