# Voice Insights: the 8-minute keynote, in the style of a Steve Jobs launch

**Same demo, same facts, same clock as `exco-8min-speaker-script.md`.** Only the delivery changes. Use that file for the checklist, the fallback table and the Q&A; use this one for what you say.

**How to deliver it in this style**

- **Short sentences. Then silence.** The pause after a line is part of the line. Where you see `[pause]`, count two seconds.
- **One idea per slide, one slide per idea.** Keep the screen empty of text. Let the product be the slide.
- **Never say "AI" first.** Talk about the *person*: the agent, the family, the evening. The technology arrives as the answer.
- **Do the demo yourself, slowly, as if it were the first time.** Wonder is the delivery.
- **Rule of three.** Lists have three items. Say them with a beat between.
- **Stand still when you reveal.** Move on the setup, freeze on the reveal.
- **Say "suggestion", never "recommendation"**, even in the flourish.

**Dictation for Act 1** (unchanged): *"I met Mr Daniel Tan today. He is thirty-eight, a sales director. He's married to Mei Ling, and they have two children, Ethan who is seven and Chloe who is four. His father, seventy, lives with them and Daniel supports him. He's worried about the home loan and the children's education. He can put aside about five hundred dollars a month."*

# ACT 1 · 0:00–2:00 · "The conversation is the product"

*(Start on a blank screen. Walk to the centre. Wait for the room.)*

**Say**

> "Every day, one of our agents sits down with a family.
>
> [pause]
>
> For an hour, they listen. A new job. A home loan. A father who is getting older. A child who starts school next year.
>
> That hour is the most valuable thing we have. It is *why we exist*.
>
> [pause]
>
> And then the agent goes home. And begins the evening.
>
> Notes. A fact-find. A record of advice. A follow-up. Typed from memory, late, and different every time.
>
> [pause]
>
> We have a name for what happens next. We lose it.
>
> The conversation is the product. And we've been throwing it away.
>
> [pause]
>
> Today, that changes.
>
> We've built something. It's called **Voice Insights**. And I want you to meet the person at the centre of it.
>
> Her name is **Juno**."

*(Open Debrief with Juno. Microphone on. Language English.)*

**Say**

> "The meeting just ended. The agent doesn't open a form. They don't open a laptop full of fields.
>
> They just *talk*."

*(Speak the dictation, slowly, warmly.)*

**Say the moment you stop**

> "Now watch. She doesn't just write it down.
>
> She tells me what she *understood*.
>
> And then she tells me what's *missing*."

*(Let Juno read back and ask her first question. Answer in one short line each time. While she asks:)*

> "Up to five questions. Short ones. Only about what matters.
>
> [pause]
>
> And look at this. Twenty-six fields of a fact-find. Filling up. As I *talk*."

> **WOW #1** *(stand still)*
> "Ninety seconds.
>
> [pause]
>
> Nobody typed a word."

# ACT 2 · 2:00–4:00 · "Eleven minds. Thirty seconds."

*(Walk. Hand on the mouse.)*

**Say**

> "Now here's where most people would say: let the AI take over.
>
> [pause]
>
> We said no.
>
> **Only a person can start the analysis.** Not the machine. The advisor."

*(Click **Approve** with a real click.)*

**Say**

> "One click.
>
> And now, eleven specialists.
>
> Needs. Risk. Affordability. Customer type. Product scoring. Our own product documents. And a compliance check.
>
> Not one chatbot. A *team*.
>
> Every step stored. Every step auditable."

*(Stay silent for 5 seconds. Let the progress run. Then:)*

> "This isn't a video. It's happening right now.
>
> Thirty seconds. Maybe forty.
>
> [pause]
>
> A suggestion like this used to take *days*."

*(Open the result. Tap one source chip.)*

**Say**

> "Look at the words. It says **suggestion**. Not recommendation. The advisor decides. We designed it that way, on purpose.
>
> And see these little chips under every sentence.
>
> Tap one. What the customer said. Or our own product document. Or the compliance check.
>
> Every sentence shows where it came from. If it can't, it says so.
>
> [pause]
>
> That's how you build something that Compliance can trust."

*(Open the Advice pack. Click Fact-find, then the follow-up message.)*

**Say**

> "And then, the evening.
>
> The fact-find. Done.
>
> The record of advice. Done.
>
> The follow-up, in the customer's language. Done.
>
> The CRM note. The brief for the next meeting. Done.
>
> The agent reviews. The agent signs. **Nothing is sent without them.**"

> **WOW #2** *(stand still)*
> "One conversation.
>
> Thirty seconds.
>
> And the evening is *theirs again*."

# ACT 3 · 4:00–6:00 · "Ask it anything"

*(Step back from the screen. Turn to the room.)*

**Say**

> "But I know what you're thinking. 'It's a black box.'
>
> [pause]
>
> So don't trust it. *Challenge* it. Out loud."

*(Open Talk to Juno. Voice on.)*

> *"Why did you put the first product first?"*

*(While she thinks:)*

> "She's reading this family's file. And our product documents. Nothing else.
>
> If it isn't there, she'll tell you."

*(After her answer, move on. Slow down.)*

**Say**

> "Now. Life changes.
>
> Customers change their minds. And sometimes the family changes.
>
> [pause]
>
> Watch this."

*(Say to Juno:)*

> *"His wife is expecting another."*

*(While she replies:)*

> "She doesn't guess.
>
> She reads back the one fact that changed. Dependants: three. To four.
>
> Three plus one is four.
>
> [pause]
>
> And here's the thing I love. **That sum is not done by the AI.** It's done by code. And she shows her working.
>
> Numbers are not something we let a model invent."

*(Click **Run it**, or say "Yes, go ahead".)*

> "And now she runs the *whole* analysis again. On a scratch copy.
>
> The real record? Untouched. This is labelled *explored*. Not real."

*(When the comparison appears:)*

> "The original. The new one. Product by product. With the reason it moved.
>
> Or the reason it didn't."

> **WOW #3** *(stand still)*
> "A new baby. One sentence.
>
> Half a minute later, it's in the analysis.
>
> [pause]
>
> The agent can *defend* this suggestion. At the table. To the customer's face."

---

# ACT 4 · 6:00–8:00 · "One more thing"

*(Switch on **Hey Juno** mode. Look at the screen until it says "Say Hey Juno". Step away from the keyboard. Hands visibly empty.)*

**Say**

> "Everything you've seen, I did with a mouse and a microphone.
>
> [pause]
>
> Now, put your hands in your pockets."

*(Say clearly, one second pause after the name:)*

> *"Hey Juno, brief me."*

*(Let her speak for about 8 to 10 seconds. Nod. Then:)*

> *"Hey Juno, stop."*

**Say**

> "A ninety-second briefing on this customer. In the car. Between meetings. Or the full report, read aloud.
>
> Any screen. Any button. No hands.
>
> [pause]
>
> And she stops when you tell her to stop."

*(If she mishears: say "Hey Juno" again, slowly, and smile. If the room is noisy, click the button, and say "in a quiet room, no hands". Don't fight it.)*

**Say, with a pause before the next line**

> "And there's... *one more thing*."

*(Say: "Hey Juno, rehearse", then "Hey Juno, start the rehearsal".)*

> "Before the next meeting, the agent rehearses.
>
> Juno plays **the customer**. This customer. Built from the agent's own debrief.
>
> She pushes back. She asks the awkward question."

*(Answer with one line as the agent. Then "Hey Juno, stop".)*

> **WOW #4** *(skip this sentence if the rehearsal did not run well)*
> "Afterwards, she coaches. What landed. What was missed.
>
> [pause]
>
> It's a flight simulator for a conversation.
>
> And the passenger is *real*."

*(Walk to the centre. Screen to the home page, or black. Slow now.)*

**Say**

> "So let's step back. What did we just do?
>
> [pause]
>
> For our **agents**: their evenings back. Take a conservative illustration. Forty-five minutes saved per case. Eight cases a month. That's six hours. Every agent. Every month.
>
> For our **customers**: a follow-up the same day, in the language they speak at home.
>
> For **Compliance**: risk caught in the conversation. Not weeks later in a file review.
>
> [pause]
>
> And for **leadership**: one picture of the whole agency force. For the first time.
>
> It's built on trust. ID and card numbers never stored. Weak speech filtered out. Juno suggests. The advisor decides.
>
> [pause]
>
> It works today. On our own documents.
>
> I'm not asking you to believe me. I'm asking for a small **pilot** with a handful of agents. So we measure the time saved on *our* numbers. And a review of the controls, with Compliance, before we go wider.
>
> [pause]
>
> The advice was always in the conversation.
>
> We just never listened.
>
> [pause]
>
> Until now.
>
> Thank you."

*(Hold the silence for three full seconds before anyone speaks.)*

## What to leave out so the style stays honest

- **No big numbers you can't prove.** The six hours is an illustration; say "illustration" aloud, as written. Don't add the 36%, 74% or 74,000-hours figures unless you have AIA's own source.
- **No "revolutionary" or "magic".** The style is understatement: simple words, confident silence. Let the room supply the adjectives.
- **Don't claim the AI "gives advice".** It gives suggestions. The advisor decides.
- **Don't say a dollar cost per conversation** unless you've checked the pre-read assumptions.

## If you run over (cut in this order)

1. The second source chip (Act 2).
2. The rehearsal (Act 4). End on Hey Juno, then go straight to "So let's step back."
3. The "why" question in Talk to Juno (Act 3); go straight to the baby.

**Never cut:** "Only a person can start the analysis", the silent wait while the agents run, and "That sum is not done by the AI."

## Three lines to memorise cold

1. *"The conversation is the product. And we've been throwing it away."*
2. *"Nobody typed a word."*
3. *"The advice was always in the conversation. We just never listened."*
