# Voice Insights: the business case in two slides

**Pre-read for the AIA Singapore Executive Committee** · CEO · CTO · COO · CFO · EXCO members

*Reading time: 5 minutes. Companion to two slides: the first covers what our insurance agents gain, the second what AIA Singapore gains.*

---

## The idea in one paragraph

Every customer conversation holds the information that decides whether someone is protected well: their family, their worries, their budget, their plans. Today most of it lives in an agent's memory and is turned into paperwork by hand, late, and unevenly. **Voice Insights turns the conversation itself into structured insight and ready-to-review documents within about half a minute**, in English, Mandarin, Malay or Tamil. Agents spend their time with customers instead of with forms. AIA gains a consistent, visible, higher-quality advice process across the whole agency force.

# Slide 1 · What our insurance agents gain

### Headline for the slide

> **"Agents get their evenings back, and customers get a better conversation."**

### The agent's day today and with Voice Insights

| | Today | With Voice Insights |
|---|---|---|
| **After the meeting** | Hours of typing: notes, fact-find, record of advice, CRM entry, follow-up message | Drafts of all five are ready to review; the agent corrects instead of writes |
| **What was said** | Reconstructed from memory; details such as a child's age or a budget can be lost | Captured from the conversation, with the customer's own words kept as evidence |
| **What was missed** | Discovered at the next meeting, or never | Listed straight away as "still to ask", so the next conversation starts with the right questions |
| **Customer follow-up** | Days later, in whatever time is left | Same day, in the customer's preferred language |
| **Confidence in the advice** | Depends on the agent's experience and recall | Every suggestion shows the reasons, the concerns and the product wording behind it |

### Five gains, in the agent's own terms

1. **More time to sell and to serve.** Admin after each meeting shrinks from a long evening task to a short review. Time recovered goes to more customer meetings, or simply to a better working week.
2. **Better conversations in the room.** A live view of the customer's family, hopes and worries builds as they speak, and the next best question is suggested. Newer agents get the benefit of a senior colleague's instinct.
3. **Fewer missed needs, so fewer missed opportunities.** The same structured view is built for every customer, which surfaces protection gaps the agent might not have raised: a spouse with no cover, children's education, a home loan.
4. **Safer, more confident advice.** Statements that could breach conduct rules, such as a promise of returns, are flagged while the conversation is happening, not weeks later in a file review. Agents protect their licence and their reputation.
5. **Serving every customer in their own language.** An agent can brief and follow up in the language the customer is comfortable with, without extra effort. This matters in a market where family decisions are often made in Mandarin, Malay or Tamil.

### What it means for the agent's income and career

- **Productivity:** more customer-facing time per week from the same working hours.
- **Quality:** a more complete record of each customer, which supports stronger, longer relationships and better persistency.
- **Growth:** new agents reach competence faster because the structure and the prompts are always available.
- **Retention of the agent:** a role less weighed down by paperwork is a better reason to stay.

A simple before/after of one agent's week: the same working hours, with the admin block shrunk and the customer-time block widened. Beside it, one real screen from the product showing the customer's picture building during a conversation.

### Suggested one-line proof point

*"An agent finishes a meeting and, within about half a minute, has a full recommendation with reasons and a follow-up draft to review."* (observed in the working prototype)

# Slide 2 · What AIA Singapore gains

### Headline for the slide

> **"A consistent, visible, and compliant advice process across every agent, at a cost per conversation of well under one dollar."**

### Six gains for the company

| Gain | What changes for AIA | Who feels it most |
|---|---|---|
| **1. Agent productivity at scale** | Hours returned to customer work across the whole agency force, without adding headcount | COO, CFO |
| **2. Higher quality and consistency** | Every conversation is analysed to the same standard, so the quality of advice no longer depends on who the agent is | CEO, COO |
| **3. Compliance caught early** | Risky statements and missing disclosures are visible during or straight after the conversation, not discovered in a later file review. Evidence for each suggestion is kept | Chief Compliance Officer, CEO |
| **4. Revenue and conversion upside** | Faster follow-up, fewer missed needs and better-prepared next meetings lift conversion and protection per customer | CEO, CFO |
| **5. Leadership visibility** | For the first time, a live view of how many conversations happen, which needs come up, how customers feel, and how long it takes to go from meeting to advice | CEO, COO |
| **6. Brand and customer trust** | A more complete, more accurate record of what the customer said, and faster, multilingual service, strengthens AIA's name as the advisor customers choose | CEO |

### The financial logic, kept simple (for the CFO)

**Value = agents using it × cases per agent × admin time saved per case × value of an hour**, plus the uplift from better conversion and fewer compliance incidents.

| Illustration | Example value |
|---|---:|
| Admin time saved per case | 45 minutes |
| Cases per agent per month | 8 |
| **Time returned per agent per month** | **6 hours** |
| Per 100 agents | 600 hours a month, about 3.75 full-time equivalents |

> **Read this as an illustration, not a forecast.** The 45 minutes and 8 cases are planning assumptions. The first step is to measure how long agents really spend today, so that every figure presented to the Committee is based on AIA's own numbers.

- **Running cost:** estimated at about SGD 0.35–0.60 per conversation in AI usage, against the value of the agent time returned. This is an estimate to be confirmed against AIA's agreements.
- **Value that is not only time:** a single avoided conduct incident, or a small lift in conversion, may outweigh the time saving.

### Strategic position

- **Differentiation:** an agency force that is faster, multilingual and better documented is hard for competitors to copy quickly.
- **A platform, not a point tool:** the same conversation intelligence can later support coaching, product feedback, and new-agent onboarding.
- **Insight from the market:** anonymised patterns in what customers ask and worry about show where product and campaign opportunities lie.

### Suggested visual

A leadership dashboard view (conversations, needs mix, sentiment, compliance flags, time to advice), beside a three-bar comparison for the CFO: time returned, conversion uplift, risk avoided.

### Suggested one-line proof point

*"Leadership sees every conversation's quality and risk in one view, instead of one sampled file at a time."*

## What the Committee should take away

1. **Agents win first.** If it saves them time and makes them better in the room, adoption follows, and the company benefit follows adoption.
2. **The company benefit is broader than time saved.** Quality, compliance, conversion, visibility and brand are where the larger value lies.
3. **The numbers need AIA's own data.** The illustrations above show how value is built; measurement turns them into a business case.

---

## Questions the Committee may ask

| Question | Short answer |
|---|---|
| Will agents actually use it? | The value to them is immediate: less typing and better customer conversations. Adoption is the thing to measure first. |
| Is the saving real or assumed? | Assumed in the illustration above; to be measured with a sample of agents before any commitment is made. |
| What does it do for compliance, honestly? | It brings risk to light earlier and keeps evidence of the reasons behind each suggestion. It supports the compliance function; it does not replace it. |
| Does it help revenue, or only cost? | Both. Time returned to selling and fewer missed needs are revenue levers, while compliance and rework are cost levers. |
| What is the downside of waiting? | Agent time keeps going to paperwork, follow-up stays slow and uneven, and risk is still found late. The value above is deferred, not lost. |
