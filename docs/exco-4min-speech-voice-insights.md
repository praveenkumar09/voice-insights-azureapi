# Voice Insights: the 4-minute EXCO speech

**For:** Group EXCO and business-unit heads. **Format:** spoken live, the demo video plays alongside.
**Length:** about 560 spoken words, plus pauses. That is 4:00 at a calm pace. Rehearse with a timer.
**Facts:** same as `exco-8min-keynote-script-jobs-style.md`. Nothing new is claimed. Say **suggestion**, never "recommendation".

## How to deliver it

- **One wow a minute.** Each minute ends on a line that lands, then silence. Count two seconds at every `[pause]`.
- **Talk about the agent, not the technology.** "AI" comes late, as the answer, never as the opener.
- **The video is the proof. You are the reason.** Never describe what is on screen. Say why it matters, then stop talking and let them see it.
- **One name.** *Spark* is the voice the agent talks to and the team of specialists behind every suggestion. Never say Juno.

## 1. The real pain · 0:00–0:50

*(Walk to the centre. Wait for the room. Start slowly.)*

> "Let me tell you about an evening.
>
> [pause]
>
> An agent has just spent an hour with a family. A home loan. A father who is getting older. A daughter starting school.
>
> That hour is the most valuable thing this company has. It is why we exist.
>
> [pause]
>
> Then the agent goes home. And starts a second job.
>
> The notes, typed from memory. The fact-find. The record of advice. The follow-up message.
>
> Late. Tired. Different every time.
>
> [pause]
>
> And three things happen. They forget what the customer said. They send a generic product, because they cannot remember what we are running this month. And they walk into the next meeting unprepared.
>
> [pause]
>
> We have a name for this. We lose it.
>
> The conversation is the product. And we have been throwing it away."

**WOW #1 · pause three seconds.** Then: "So we built Voice Insights." *(Start the video.)*

---

## 2. The debrief: why this is not a note-taker · 0:50–1:35

*(Video: Spark debrief. Speak over the first seconds, then let the video run.)*

> "The meeting ends. The agent does not open a form. They just talk.
>
> [pause]
>
> Now, you have seen this before. A recorder that gives you a transcript. A knowledge assistant that answers when you ask.
>
> Both wait for the agent to do the work.
>
> **Spark doesn't wait.**
>
> She listens, in English, in Mandarin, in Malay, in Tamil, even mixed in the same sentence, the way Singapore actually talks.
>
> She tells the agent what she understood. Then she tells them what is **missing**. Five short questions, only about what matters. *Is the budget monthly or yearly? Is the father a dependant?*
>
> [pause]
>
> A transcript is words. A copilot is an answer. **A debrief is a completed file.**
>
> Twenty-six fields of the fact-find, filling up as the agent speaks.
>
> [pause]
>
> And only a person can approve. Not the machine."

**WOW #2 · stand still.** "Ninety seconds. Nobody typed a word."

---

## 3. Spark: product and campaign suggestions · 1:35–2:10

*(Video: the agents running, then the suggestion page with the campaign card.)*

> "One click from the agent. And behind Spark, a team goes to work.
>
> Not one chatbot. A team of specialists. Needs. Risk. Affordability. Product fit. Our own product documents. Compliance.
>
> [pause]
>
> And one more. The **campaign agent**.
>
> Marketing uploads a poster. Spark reads it. An admin checks it, and it goes live, with dates and a business unit.
>
> Then in a conversation, a customer says: *'I want to stay fit.'* He never says the word Vitality. Spark understands the meaning, and puts the right campaign on the page, with his own words as the reason.
>
> [pause]
>
> Every campaign we spend money on finally reaches the right customer, in the right moment.
>
> If nothing fits, it shows nothing. We do not push.
>
> [pause]
>
> And every sentence shows where it came from. The customer. Our product document. Or the compliance check. If it cannot show a source, it says so."

**WOW #3 · stand still.** "It doesn't just know our products. It knows what we are running today."

---

## 4. Proposal and the complete advice pack · 2:10–2:50

*(Video: proposal, then the Advice Pack tabs.)*

> "Now the evening, the one we started with.
>
> A proposal for the customer. Ready.
>
> And behind it, the complete advice pack.
>
> The fact-find. The record of advice. The follow-up, in the language the family speaks at home. The CRM note. The brief for the next meeting.
>
> [pause]
>
> Five documents. Produced from the one conversation. Not typed. Not remembered.
>
> The agent reads. The agent edits. The agent signs. **Nothing leaves without them.**
>
> [pause]
>
> And for Compliance, this is the part that matters. The file is complete the same day, in the same shape, every time. Risk is caught in the conversation. Not weeks later in a file review."

**WOW #4 · stand still.** "One conversation. Thirty seconds. And the evening is theirs again."

---

## 5. The rehearsal: why it is not a coaching bot · 2:50–3:35

*(Video: Dress Rehearsal. Spark plays the customer.)*

> "Now, the next meeting.
>
> Every company has coaching. Role-play. A trainer. A bot with a script.
>
> Generic customers. Generic objections. Generic feedback.
>
> [pause]
>
> **Spark plays this customer.**
>
> Built from the agent's own debrief. His budget. His worry about the loan. His father. The thing he hesitated over last time.
>
> She pushes back, the way **he** would push back.
>
> [pause]
>
> Then she coaches the agent on what landed, and what was missed. Against **his** facts. Not a textbook.
>
> [pause]
>
> The agent walks in on Thursday having already had the difficult conversation. Twice."

**WOW #5 · stand still.** "It is a flight simulator for a conversation. And the passenger is real."

---

## 6. Challenge Spark, and the close · 3:35–4:00

*(Video: the "why this first?" question and the what-if. Speak slowly.)*

> "One last thing. And it's short.
>
> A suggestion you cannot question is a black box. So we built it to be challenged.
>
> Ask Spark why. Out loud. It answers from this family's file and our documents, with the source.
>
> Say *'his wife is expecting another.'* Spark re-runs the whole analysis, on a copy, in half a minute. The real record is untouched. It shows what moved, and why.
>
> [pause]
>
> The agent can **defend** the suggestion, at the table, to the customer's face.
>
> [pause]
>
> Spark suggests. The advisor decides.
>
> [pause]
>
> The advice was always in the conversation.
>
> We just never listened.
>
> [pause]
>
> Until now."

*(Hold the silence three seconds before anyone speaks.)*

## Keep it honest (EXCO will test every claim)

- **No numbers you cannot source.** If asked for time saved, use the illustration from the 8-minute script: 45 minutes a case, 8 cases a month, 6 hours per agent. Say "illustration" aloud.
- **Never say the AI "advises" or "recommends".** It suggests. The advisor decides. Approval is a real human click.
- **Do not name a language Spark has not been tested in as "perfect".** English and Mandarin are tested; Malay and Tamil work but Tamil and Malay Ask answers are less tested. Say "understands", not "flawless".
- **"The campaign is live" means an admin reviewed it.** Marketing uploads, an admin approves. Say it that way if asked.
- **Challenge Spark:** if "Challenge Spark" is a named button on your final build, say its name at 3:35. This script describes the Ask and What-if features that exist today.

## If you run over, cut in this order

1. The second example question in the debrief (Part 2).
2. "If nothing fits, it shows nothing" (Part 3). Keep the line before it.
3. The Compliance paragraph in Part 4.

**Never cut:** "We lose it." · "Nobody typed a word." · "Spark plays this customer." · "And the passenger is real." · "We just never listened."
